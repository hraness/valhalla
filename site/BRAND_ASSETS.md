# Vhalla website identity

The favicon silhouette comes from the actual header mark, `valhalla-mark.svg`. Browser variants render it in pure white on transparency. Apple touch variants use the same white mark on black. The visible bounds are centered horizontally and vertically and fill the square on their longest axis, preserving the aspect ratio with no added padding.

Social cards generated by `bun run generate:og` (`generate-og.tsx`, satori +
resvg) from the shared `@hraness/web-discovery` social-image template. The one
`defineSocialImageSite` declaration in `social-cards.ts` supplies the name,
tagline, domain, the header's `valhalla-mark.svg` (painted in foil beside the
name, as in the header) and the `rose-pine` Design Kit palette, so each card
looks like the site's header and hero. The home card sets the hero's eyebrow over
the tagline, which is also the hero heading; each collection card adds only its page copy. `social.png` is the home card; the others belong to the
